# Finance Q4 Risk Report

The finance department identified delayed invoice approvals in the enterprise
procurement workflow. The largest exposure is in cloud infrastructure renewals,
where three contracts require executive approval before the end of Q4.

Projected Q4 revenue remains on plan, but margin pressure increased after emergency
security remediation work. Finance recommends holding discretionary travel spend
until vendor consolidation is complete.

This report is restricted to finance and executive roles because it includes revenue,
vendor, and contract exposure details.
